aKlima — signing in

You only have to do this once per phone.

What aKlima is

A small app for our two Hisense air conditioners — the ones already in the ConnectLife app. It does the same job without the big vendor app: on/off, temperature, mode, fan, quiet, swing.

Why the sign-in works this way

The ConnectLife account was created with Google. Google does not allow its sign-in to run inside another app's built-in page, so the sign-in happens once in your normal browser, and the app receives a one-time code from it. After that the app stays signed in and never asks again.

The steps

  1. Open aKlima and tap Sign in.
  2. Tap Sign in with Google (opens a browser).
  3. Tap Open in browser — this opens Chrome, Samsung Internet, or whatever browser you use.
  4. On the ConnectLife page, tap SIGN IN BY GOOGLE and choose your Google account.
  5. The browser then shows a page it cannot load — something like “This site can't be reached” for homeassistant.local. That is expected. It is a placeholder address; nothing is broken.
  6. Copy that address from the address bar:
    • Chrome / Android: long-press the address bar, then Copy
    • Safari / iPhone: tap the address bar, then Copy
  7. Switch back to aKlima, tap Paste code, paste the address, tap Sign in.
  8. Done — the app opens on your air conditioners.
What you are copying: the address always starts with homeassistant.local and contains code= in the middle. If what you paste has no code in it, the app simply says so and nothing happens — you can try again.

If the browser will not open

  1. In aKlima, tap Copy link.
  2. Send that link to yourself — Telegram “Saved Messages” is the easiest — and open it on a laptop or a tablet.
  3. Sign in with Google there. You land on the same unloadable page: copy its address.
  4. Send that address back to the phone and paste it into Paste code.

Using it afterwards

  • Power: the big round button.
  • Temperature: − and + next to the number.
  • Mode, fan, quiet, swing, eco, turbo, sleep: the ⋯ button, top right.
  • Two homes: one chip per unit at the top (Vogosca, Svrake).
  • Sign out: also in the ⋯ menu.

Good to know

  • The address you copy contains a one-time code. If you take too long it expires — just repeat the steps.
  • Do this once per phone. Each phone keeps its own private session — signing in on one phone does not sign the others out.
  • aKlima never sees your Google password: the sign-in happens on Google's and ConnectLife's own pages, in your browser.
  • Nothing changes on the air conditioner until you actually press something in the app.
  • The app shows the reason, in plain text, if anything fails — screenshot it and send it on.
